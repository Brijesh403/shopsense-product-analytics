# Experiment Ideas — Beyond the Cart-Abandonment Nudge

Three follow-up experiments the findings across this project point to,
beyond the cart-abandonment discount nudge already tested in
[`ab_test_simulation.md`](ab_test_simulation.md).

## Experiment 1 — At-Risk Win-Back Email

*Hypothesis:* A targeted win-back email to the 65,234 At-Risk users will
recover a measurable share of them into repeat buyers.

*Baseline:* At-Risk users currently generate $1.62M (25.5% of revenue)
with no active retention effort.

*Measurement:* A/B test — control (no email) vs. treatment (win-back
email with a modest incentive). Primary metric: 30-day repeat purchase
rate within the At-Risk segment.

## Experiment 2 — Promo Timing Shift to Peak Hours

*Hypothesis:* Shifting promotional pushes to the 9 AM–1 PM peak
conversion window (vs. spreading them evenly through the day) will lift
same-day conversion at zero additional spend.

*Baseline:* Peak-hour conversion (~6%) is nearly double the overnight
rate (~3.5%).

*Measurement:* Compare conversion rate on promo days before vs. after
the timing shift, holding promo budget constant.

## Experiment 3 — December Revenue Drop Root Cause

*Hypothesis:* The -29.6% December revenue drop is a campaign/inventory
gap, not genuine demand softening.

*Baseline:* November revenue peaked at $1.53M; December fell to $1.08M
with no corresponding drop in traffic-side metrics.

*Measurement:* Not an A/B test — a diagnostic deep-dive comparing
December's funnel-stage conversion rates and top-brand availability
against November's, to isolate whether the drop is demand, supply, or
promotional.
