# Resume Highlights — ShopSense Product Analytics

**Project header (for the Projects section of a resume):**

> **ShopSense — Product Funnel & Retention Analytics**
> MySQL · Python · Power BI · github.com/Brijesh403/shopsense-product-analytics

**Bullet points (select 3–4):**

- Analyzed 20.7M ecommerce events from 1.6M users in MySQL to identify funnel drop-off points, isolating cart abandonment as the primary revenue gap, with 72% of users who added items to cart failing to complete a purchase.

- Developed a SQL cohort retention model showing that the October cohort retained at 18% in month one against 8–10% for later cohorts, indicating growth driven by a single acquisition spike rather than sustained retention.

- Performed RFM segmentation that identified 14,000 high-value "Champion" customers (13% of buyers, averaging $215 each) and flagged 59% of buyers as at-risk, informing a targeted win-back recommendation.

- Identified a conversion peak of approximately 6% between 9 AM and 1 PM versus 3.5% overnight, providing a no-cost opportunity to optimise promotional timing.

- Delivered a four-page Power BI dashboard and a one-page executive summary, enabling non-technical stakeholders to act on the findings without querying the data directly.
