# 📋 Business Questions Tracker — ShopSense

All questions below were answered using SQL analysis on 20.7M+ behavioral events.

---

## ✅ Funnel & Conversion

**1. Where are users dropping off?**
> 75% drop at the view-to-cart stage — only 24.93% of viewers add to cart.

**2. What is the cart abandonment rate?**
> 72.48% — 7 in every 10 cart users never complete a purchase.

**3. When do users convert most?**
> 9 AM–1 PM is the peak window; 11 AM converts highest at 6.04%.

**4. Which brands convert best?**
> eunyul (51.32%), severina (49.28%), elskin (45.76%) — well above the 6.92% average.

**5. What is the overall conversion rate?**
> 6.92% overall | 24.93% view-to-cart | 27.75% cart-to-purchase.

---

## ✅ Retention & Cohorts

**6. What is Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30 retention?**
> Day 1: 1.12% | Day 7: 4.16% | Day 30: 11.95%.

**7. Which cohort retains users best?**
> October 2019 — 18.49% Month-1 retention, nearly 2x the platform average.
> November 2019 had the highest acquisition (26,761 new buyers).

---

## ✅ Revenue & Value

**8. What is ARPU by cohort?**
> October cohort generated the highest lifetime ARPU at $82.45 vs February's $41.93.

**9. What is the monthly revenue trend?**
> Revenue peaked in November ($1.53M) and dropped 29.6% in December — a key seasonality red flag.

**10. Which brands drive the most revenue?**
> runail ($343K) led on volume; "strong" drove $152K from just 788 buyers ($178 AOV — a premium segment).

## 💡 Top Recommendations for Product Team

1. **Attack cart abandonment** — retarget the 72% who abandon within 2 hours; test an exit-intent offer.
2. **Re-engage At-Risk users** — 59% of buyers are at churn risk; a targeted win-back campaign protects existing revenue.
3. **Investigate the December drop** — revenue fell 29.6% in a month that should be strong; likely a campaign or stock issue.
4. **Time promotions for 9 AM–1 PM** — the proven peak conversion window.
5. **Promote high-converting brands** — give eunyul, severina, and elskin premium placement.
